Meta Image Tool

Convert and prepare JPEG images directly in your browser.

Processing happens locally in your browser. The output is normalized for orientation, center-cropped proportionally to 3:4, resized to exactly 3024 × 4032, and given a minimal JPEG EXIF block.

iPhone: HEIC/HEIF support depends on the browser. Safari on supported Apple devices can usually decode it. If it cannot, export the photo as JPEG or use Most Compatible camera format.
